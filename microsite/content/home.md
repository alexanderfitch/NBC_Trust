That's usually the first thing an owner assumes, so it's worth saying first. NBC Trust is a Nebraska trust company. We built the Owner's Table because good advisors deserve better options than a roll-up or a sunset.

### No acquisition, no roll-up

Nobody here is putting a multiple on your revenue or folding your clients into a call center.

::: faq tint
^ Questions owners ask

## Fair questions, straight answers.

### Why did I get an email saying I have an account?

We set up free memberships for independent advisors in our region who we thought would find this useful. Your seat is just a placeholder until you claim it. If you'd rather not, ignore the email or click "remove me" and we won't contact you again.

### Is this a sales funnel?

It's a library and a community first. NBC Trust does help advisors plan successions, and we'd be glad to talk when you're ready. But the guides and tools are useful on their own, and most members never raise their hand. That's fine with us.

### Who is NBC Trust?

A trust company based in Lincoln, Nebraska, working with families and the advisors who serve them. One of our own advisors went through his own handover here. That's where this started.

### What happens to my information?

We use it to send what you asked for, and nothing else. We never sell or share it, and you can delete your membership at any time.

### I'm years away from stepping back. Is this for me?

Especially. The owners with the most options are the ones who start early, and almost everything in the library pays you back this year, long before any handover.
