# Where does your week actually go?

Tick every task you still do yourself, even sometimes. The total is a conservative estimate of the time you could hand to a team member, a service, or a system without your clients noticing anything but faster answers.

[partial:hour-audit]

::: tiles tint
^ What to do with the number

## You don't need to hand it all off. Just the first three hours.

### Pick one task this week

The easiest to describe, not the biggest. Write down how you do it in plain steps.

[The $50 task test](/library/the-fifty-dollar-task-test/)

### Give it to someone for a month

A team member, a virtual assistant, or a piece of software. Check in weekly, then let go.

[Meeting prep in fifteen minutes](/library/meeting-prep-in-fifteen-minutes/)

### Keep the instructions

Those plain steps are the first page of your operating manual. It's worth more than you think.

[Write the manual in your head](/library/write-the-manual-in-your-head/)
