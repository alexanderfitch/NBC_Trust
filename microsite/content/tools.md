# Ten minutes, a cup of coffee, and an honest look at your firm.

Both tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you tick is saved or sent anywhere, and you don't need to sign in to use them.

::: tiles compact

### The hour audit

Twelve common tasks that quietly fill an owner's week. Tick the ones you still do yourself and see how many hours a week, and a year, you could hand to someone else.

-> [Start the hour audit](/tools/hour-audit/)

### The readiness check

Twelve plain statements about you, your clients, your operations and your plan. A clear picture of what would need doing before someone else could run your firm.

-> [Take the readiness check](/tools/readiness/)
:::
