# Ready to talk about what comes next? We're here.

Whether you're a year away or ten, the first conversation is just that: a conversation about what you want for yourself, your clients and the name you built. No proposal, no pressure, and it stays between us.

[partial:hand-form]

::: checks tint
^ What we won't do

## A few promises, in writing.

### We won't rush you

Some of our best conversations happen once a year for several years. You set the pace.

### We won't contact your clients or team

Until you say so, nobody outside the conversation knows it is happening.

### We won't make you an offer you didn't ask for

We are here to help you plan, not to put a price on your life's work.
:::
