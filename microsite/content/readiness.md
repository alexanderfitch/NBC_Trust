# Could your practice run without you?

Not "should it", and not "will it". Just: if you stepped away for a while, what would hold and what wouldn't? Tick each statement that is true today. There are no wrong answers and nothing is saved.

^ Reading your score

## Readiness isn't a decision. It's options.

A high score doesn't mean you should step back. It means that when you choose to, you'll be choosing between good options rather than taking whatever is left.

### If you scored low

Start with the operations questions. They pay off immediately, in hours, and they are the foundation everything else sits on.

[Write the manual in your head](/library/write-the-manual-in-your-head/)

### If you scored in the middle

Look at the client questions. Introducing your clients to the rest of your team is the slowest work and the most valuable.

[Telling your clients](/library/telling-your-clients/)

### If you scored high

You have time to think clearly about what you want. Read the honest comparison of the ways owners exit, then decide whether to talk.

[Five ways owners exit](/library/five-ways-owners-exit/)
