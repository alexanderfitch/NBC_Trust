## Good questions to ask.

### Why do I already have an account?

We reserved free memberships for independent advisors in our region who we thought would find this useful. Nothing is active until you claim it, and if you don't, we'll delete it.

### Will someone call me?

Not because you joined. We'll only reach out personally if you tell us you're ready for a conversation, here or later from your account.

### What does it cost?

Nothing, now or later. NBC Trust runs the Owner's Table because good advisors deserve good options, and because some members eventually want to talk with us about succession.

### Can I leave?

Any time, with one click, and we delete your details.
