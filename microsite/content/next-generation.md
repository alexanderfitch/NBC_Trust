# Someone has to be on the other end of this.

Every succession story needs two people. The advisor stepping back gets most of the attention. The one taking over is the reason it works.

[video:morgans-view]

## What it actually asks of you

Not capital, in the first instance. Patience, and a genuine willingness to inherit relationships on their terms rather than yours.

If you are building a practice and would consider taking on someone else's, that is a conversation worth having earlier than you'd think, because the good handovers take years, not months.
